Frequently Asked Questions

What email type converts best? ▼

It depends on your goal. Welcome emails have the highest open rates (often 50-80%), making them ideal for relationship building. Abandoned cart emails drive direct revenue with recovery rates of 5-10%. Promotional emails work best for time-sensitive offers, while newsletters build long-term engagement and trust with your audience.

How long should a marketing email be? ▼

50-125 words for promotional emails, 150-300 words for newsletters. Shorter emails typically have higher click rates because they respect the reader's time and focus attention on a single call to action. However, the ideal length depends on your audience and the complexity of your offer — test different lengths to find what works best.

What makes a good subject line? ▼

The best subject lines use personalization, curiosity, urgency, or a clear benefit to compel opens. Keep it under 50 characters for mobile optimization. Avoid spam trigger words like "free" or "act now" in all caps. Testing emoji usage, questions, and power words can significantly improve your open rates over time.
