AI has become one of the most powerful learning tools available today. Whether you're a student trying to grasp a difficult concept, a professional exploring a new field, or simply curious about how something works, AI can provide clear, personalized explanations on virtually any topic. The key to getting a great explanation is crafting the right prompt — and that's exactly what this tool helps you do.

The Feynman Technique, named after Nobel Prize-winning physicist Richard Feynman, is a learning method based on the idea that if you can't explain something simply, you don't truly understand it. This principle is at the heart of our tool: by asking AI to explain topics at various complexity levels, using analogies and examples, you can build a deep and intuitive understanding of any subject. The ELI5 option is particularly effective for breaking through the initial confusion around complex topics.

Clear explanations matter because they save time and reduce frustration. Instead of reading through dense textbooks or scouring multiple articles, you can get a well-structured, appropriately-leveled explanation in seconds. Whether you need a quick overview or a detailed breakdown, this prompt generator gives AI the right instructions to deliver exactly what you need for effective learning.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does ELI5 mean? ▼

ELI5 stands for "Explain Like I'm 5." It's a popular internet term that means breaking down a complex topic into the simplest possible language, as if you were explaining it to a five-year-old. When you select this option, the generated prompt instructs the AI to use very simple words, everyday analogies, and short sentences to make even the most advanced topics accessible to anyone.

Can this explain technical subjects? ▼

Absolutely. This tool works for any subject — from quantum physics and machine learning to economics and philosophy. You can choose your complexity level to match your current understanding. For technical subjects, the "Intermediate" or "Advanced" options will preserve the necessary technical depth while still keeping the explanation organized and clear.

How is this different from the general prompt builder? ▼

The general prompt builder is a flexible, all-purpose tool for any type of AI prompt. This "Explain a Topic" generator is specifically optimized for learning and comprehension. It includes options tailored to educational needs — like complexity levels, analogies, examples, and output formats — and generates prompts that instruct the AI to act as a teacher, producing structured explanations designed to maximize understanding.
