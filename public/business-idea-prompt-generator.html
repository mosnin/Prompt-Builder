Frequently Asked Questions

Can AI really generate good business ideas? ▼

AI excels at combining patterns across industries to surface unique business opportunities. It can analyze market gaps, trending niches, and proven business models to generate ideas you might not have considered. Use AI for brainstorming and initial concept development, then validate with real market research, customer conversations, and competitive analysis before committing resources.

What's the best low-cost business to start? ▼

Service businesses like consulting, freelancing, and coaching have the lowest startup costs since they primarily require your time and expertise. Digital products such as online courses, templates, and ebooks scale best because they have near-zero marginal costs. The best choice depends on your skills, available time, and long-term goals — our generator helps match ideas to your specific situation.

How do I validate a business idea? ▼

Start by talking to potential customers to understand their pain points and willingness to pay. Check search volume using tools like Google Trends to gauge demand. Analyze competitors to see if the market is viable but not oversaturated. Finally, test with a minimum viable product — a simple landing page, pre-sale, or prototype — before building the full solution.
