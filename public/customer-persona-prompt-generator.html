Frequently Asked Questions

What is a customer persona? ▼

A customer persona is a semi-fictional representation of your ideal customer based on market research and real data about your existing customers. It includes demographics, behavior patterns, motivations, goals, and pain points. Personas help businesses make more informed decisions about product development, marketing strategies, and customer service by giving teams a clear picture of who they're serving.

How many personas do I need? ▼

Most businesses benefit from 2-4 personas. Too many dilutes your marketing focus and makes it difficult to create targeted campaigns. Start with your primary customer segment, then add additional personas only if they represent meaningfully different buying behaviors or needs. Each persona should drive distinct marketing strategies — if two personas would receive the same messaging, they should be combined into one.

How do I validate my persona? ▼

Interview real customers, analyze your CRM data, run surveys, and check if your marketing resonates better when targeted to the persona. Compare the AI-generated persona against your actual customer data — look at purchase history, support interactions, and engagement metrics. If the persona doesn't match reality, refine it. The best personas are living documents that evolve as you gather more customer insights.
